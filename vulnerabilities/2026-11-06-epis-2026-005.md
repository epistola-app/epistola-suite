# Removing a user's identity-provider roles does not remove their tenant access

At an OIDC login the suite takes a user's tenant roles from the token's `groups` and flat-role
claims, and stores them in `tenant_memberships`. When a token carried no tenant and no global roles,
the login fell back to the roles stored by the user's earlier logins. Nothing removes those stored
roles when the identity provider takes them away, so the fallback was always the user's roles as of
their last login that still carried some.

## Impact

Taking every Epistola group or role away from a user at the identity provider, while their account
there stays able to sign in, did not revoke their access. At their next login they received the
tenant roles they last held, administration included, in every tenant they had been a member of,
for as long as the account could sign in. A token carrying only platform roles fell back the same
way. Removing some of a user's roles worked: a token with any tenant or global role replaced the
stored set.

The typical case is a user who changes team or department, keeps their single-sign-on account, and
is taken out of the Epistola groups. Local (form) login, API keys, bearer tokens and demo mode are not
affected.

## Severity

This vulnerability is rated **Medium**, CVSS 3.1 score **6.8**
(`CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:H/PR:L/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:N`). The retained roles may be administrative, so
confidentiality and integrity within the tenant are high. Attack complexity is high because the
attacker relies on the operator revoking access by removing groups while leaving the account able to
sign in, a condition outside the attacker's control.

## Affected versions

Introduced in 0.6.0 by `2e7d4b2bb5feb8c3cce123f870d970ff63c51419`, which began storing the token's
memberships while keeping the stored-membership fallback. Every release from 0.6.0 through 1.5.0 is
affected, and the 2.0 pre-releases 2.0.0-alpha.1 and 2.0.0-beta.1.

## Resolution

Fixed in **1.5.1** (`93c4146f2609b8152b0af0229afad1b81e4cbbc9`) and on the 2.0 line in
`f9d936f52cbdbf4923ddda68988a3042928a9b81`, which ships in the next 2.0 pre-release.

The token is authoritative for an OIDC login: it grants exactly the roles the identity provider sent,
and the stored memberships are never a fallback. A login without tenant or global roles grants no
tenant access; the rows stored by earlier logins stay in `tenant_memberships`, but an OIDC login no
longer reads them. Demo mode's login resolver, which assigns a personal tenant when the token carries
no roles, is unchanged.

Upgrade to 1.5.1. If you cannot upgrade yet, disable the user's account at the identity provider
rather than only removing their groups, or delete the user's rows from `tenant_memberships` after
removing them.
