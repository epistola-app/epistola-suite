Disconnect from Exchange

Revokes the tenant connection in Exchange and removes its credentials from Suite. The Exchange application, publication history and immutable catalog namespace bindings are kept, so reconnecting later restores the same identity. Queued publications that have not been accepted are marked failed and can be retried after reconnecting.

Exchange is unreachable

If Exchange cannot be reached at all, you can forget the local credentials without revoking the remote connection. Only use this when you are able to revoke the connection in Exchange yourself — otherwise it keeps granting access.
