This installation counts, for every tenant and hour, how many documents it generated and how many previews it rendered. With this tenant's agreement, every hour the counts of the hour before go to Epistola, with the running totals, and Epistola bills the installation's operator by the documents generated. Previews are counted, not billed.

Only the numbers are sent, with the tenant's key, or its reference when this installation keeps its tenants confidential. No document, template, data or person is sent.
