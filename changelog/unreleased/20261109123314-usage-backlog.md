title: How far back missed usage hours are still reported is configurable, and old bookkeeping is pruned.
---

`epistola.support.usage.backlog` (Helm `support.usage.backlog`, default `72h`, at least an hour) sets how
far back hours missed while the installation or the support service was down are still reported for
billing once both are back. The record of which hours went out is now kept only as long as a report can
look back, plus a day, instead of forever.
