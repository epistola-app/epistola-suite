title: Release images are published for linux/arm64 as well as linux/amd64.
---

The `epistola-suite` image tags (`:<version>`, `:latest` and their `-demo` counterparts) are now
multi-arch: the same tag runs natively on arm64 nodes, such as AWS Graviton or Apple Silicon. Each
architecture is built on its own runner. The signature and the SBOM attestation are on the
multi-arch image, so `cosign verify` and `cosign verify-attestation` work as before.
