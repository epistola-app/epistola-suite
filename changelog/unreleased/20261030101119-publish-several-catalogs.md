---
type: feat
scopes: [catalog, ui]
audience: user
title: Several catalogs can be published at once, each after the catalogs it uses.
---

_Publish several…_ on the catalogs list shows every catalog with something to publish, the version
each becomes and what it publishes along, and publishes the ticked ones in one go, dependencies first.
Catalogs that build on each other, as those upgraded from 1.x do, no longer need publishing one by one
in the right order.
