title: The upgrade guide says releases render with 2.0's rendering defaults.
---

A 1.x version rendered with the defaults it was published with, or 1.x's own when it recorded none; a release cut in 2.0 renders with V8, so spacing can change. `docs/upgrades.md` lists it among the reasons documents can look different, with what to do where a layout must stay.
