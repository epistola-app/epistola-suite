title: Support requests go to a partner connection, and its replies come back.
---

Who handles new support requests can be a partner connection, and the support desk can pass a request
on to one. A request goes to a partner while the tenant shares support requests with it; when it is
handed over, what it holds so far goes along, except internal notes, and afterwards what is written
while the tenant still shares with that partner. Replies, status and priority come back from the
partner as from Epistola support.
