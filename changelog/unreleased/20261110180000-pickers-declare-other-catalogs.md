title: Pickers offer other catalogs' resources apart, and using one asks to add the dependency first.
---

Every picker (stencils, images and fonts in the editor; themes, attributes and code lists in the
pages) lists the resources of the catalogs a catalog declares, and, apart from them, those of the
tenant's other catalogs, marked "adds a dependency". Choosing one of those asks whether to make the
catalog depend on it; confirmed, the dependency is added and the choice goes through, declined,
nothing changes. A catalog that can be changed here is added at its next version, one Epistola
supplies at its highest release. Nothing is offered apart where it could not be added: in a release,
in a catalog that cannot be changed here, or without permission to edit templates.
