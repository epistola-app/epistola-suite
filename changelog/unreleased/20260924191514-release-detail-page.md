title: A catalog release has a page showing what it contained.
---

A catalog page shows its resources and its details — name, description, keywords — but those belong
to a version, not to the catalog, and only the working copy's were ever shown. Each release now has
a page of its own, reached from the release list, showing the resources it contained and the details
it carried at the time. So a release made under an old name still says so.

A release cut before Epistola kept the content of a release shows its details and says its resources
cannot be listed, rather than appearing to have contained nothing.
