type: fix
scopes: [generation]
audience: user
title: A lineHeight below 1 no longer drops the last line of a page header or footer.
---

When a page header or footer was sized to its content and the text had a `lineHeight` below 1, the
last line — often the page number — was left out of the PDF without an error. From rendering
defaults V6, used by newly published versions, it is drawn, and nothing else in the band moves.
Versions published earlier keep rendering as they did.
