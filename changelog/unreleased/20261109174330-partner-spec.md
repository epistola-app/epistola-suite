title: The support connection protocol for partners is published.
---

`docs/support-partner-api.yaml` (OpenAPI) and `docs/support-partners.md` describe what a partner
implements to take support requests and usage from installations: the token, the connection check,
requests with their messages, attachments and changes (including escalation), usage per hour and the
discovery document. Its operations are those of the Epistola support service, so one connector serves
both.
