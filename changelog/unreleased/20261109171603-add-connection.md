title: Installation administrators add, change and remove support connections on the platform page.
---

Under Platform → Epistola support → Other support connections, an installation administrator adds a
connection by looking up a partner's domain, which fills in what it publishes, or by filling in its
endpoint, and enters the token the partner issued. A connection can be renamed, have its services and
token changed, or be removed; requests it handled return to the support desk. What an administrator
saved is no longer replaced by the configuration at the next start.
