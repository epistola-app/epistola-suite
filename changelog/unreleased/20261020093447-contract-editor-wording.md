title: The data contract editor no longer shows a red error on opening, and its Save button no longer says "draft".
---

Opening a contract for editing showed "Add at least one test data example before saving" in red before anything was changed; it now appears once there is something to save. The save controls said "Save draft", "Draft saved" and "Unsaved draft changes", although they save the contract in the version being worked on, and "draft" means a catalog version elsewhere; they now say "Save", "Saved" and "Unsaved changes".
