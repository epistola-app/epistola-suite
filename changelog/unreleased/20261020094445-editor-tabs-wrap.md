title: The editor's side panel shows all its tabs, wrapping onto a second line, instead of cutting off Document and hiding Quality.

With the Quality or AI tab switched on, the side panel's tab strip cut "Document" off and pushed the last tabs out of sight; the arrows to scroll to them appeared only on hover. The tabs now wrap onto a second line when they do not fit, so every tab is in sight.
