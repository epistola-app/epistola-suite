title: A review of why AI-assisted work has become less effective.
---

The architecture core is healthy, but the layer agents work through has drifted: `CLAUDE.md` grew 17× in nine months without a test, all six code-writing skills teach removed or banned patterns, CI skips checks the docs call enforced, one guard test cannot fire, and the 738 KB changelog is edited by 85% of commits. The record proposes a canonical slim `AGENTS.md` with area guides and path-scoped rule cards, changelog fragments, hooks, a skill portfolio, and a check that tests the instructions themselves.
