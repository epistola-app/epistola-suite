scopes: [editor]
title: The editor's unit menus explain "sp" and "pt" when you point at them.
---

Spacing, size and border fields offered the units "pt" and "sp" without saying what "sp" is. Pointing at the unit menu or one of its choices now says it: "Spacing steps from the theme: 1 sp = 4 pt" (with the theme's own step) and "Points: 1 pt = 1/72 inch".
