title: An assistant can deploy and undeploy catalog releases over MCP.
---

`deploy_release` deploys a release, or a draft to serve it live, to an environment, beside the
catalog's other releases there or in their place; `undeploy_release` stops serving one. Both need
`ENVIRONMENT_DEPLOY`, refuse what the environment page refuses, and return the environment as
`list_environments` shows it. Generating documents is still not offered over MCP: `preview_document`
is the only rendering.
