title: The REST API implements contract 2.0, built on catalog versions, and MCP follows it.
---

Template versions, variant drafts, contract drafts, stencil versions and environment activations are gone from the REST API. Every catalog resource is reached under `/tenants/{tenantKey}/catalogs/{registry}/{namespace}/{catalogKey}/versions/{catalogVersion}/…`, with `~` for the installation's default registry and `{catalogVersion}` being `next`, the id of long-term work, or a published version number; writes need a draft and are refused with 409 on a published version, whose reads answer with what it holds. `key` is the only address of a resource; the `slug` and `id` twins are gone. A variant's and a stencil's content is read and replaced through `/content`, and each reports a status: new, modified, ready or released. Catalog versions: `listCatalogVersions`, `getCatalogVersion`, `getCatalogChanges`, `markVariantReady`, `markTemplateReady`, `markStencilReady`, `markCatalogReady`, `createCatalogDraft`, `renameCatalogDraft`, `deleteCatalogDraft`, `promoteCatalogDraft`, `previewCatalogDraftRelease`, `releaseCatalogDraft`, `listCatalogDependencies` and `updateCatalogDependencies`. Environments: `deployRelease` (with `includeDependencies`), `undeployRelease`, `listEnvironmentDeployments`, `listDeploymentHistory`, `setEnvironmentOverride`, `clearEnvironmentOverride`, `listEnvironmentOverrides` and `EnvironmentDto.production`. Generation, batches, preview and data validation take `version` (an exact version or a range) and `environmentKey`, and preview also `draft`; jobs, documents and collected results report `releaseVersion` instead of `versionId`, or `draftId` and `draftLabel` for a document made from work in progress. `listStencilUsage` reports `fits` and `problems` per template variant, and a breaking data-model update is no longer refused (`forceUpdate` has no effect). `importCatalog` accepts a bundle exported with its dependencies; importing under another name or into a draft answers 501. Over MCP the version and publish tools (`publish_template_version`, `publish_stencil_version`, `publish_data_contract`, `update_template_draft`, `update_stencil_draft`, `list_versions`, `get_version`, `list_stencil_versions`, `get_stencil_version`, `upgrade_stencil_in_template`) give way to `update_variant_content`, the `mark_*_ready` tools, `list_catalog_versions`, `get_catalog_changes`, `start_long_term_work`, `rename_long_term_work`, `promote_long_term_work`, `discard_catalog_work`, `preview_release_number`, `release_next_version` and the catalog dependency tools; content tools take a `catalogVersion` (`next` or a draft id), and `preview_document` and `analyze_template_data` take `releaseVersion` and `environmentId`. **Action on upgrade:** move REST clients and MCP prompts to contract 2.0.
