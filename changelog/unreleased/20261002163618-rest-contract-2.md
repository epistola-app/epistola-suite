title: The REST API implements contract 2.0, built on catalog releases.
---

Template versions, variant drafts, environment activations, stencil versions and the separate
contract draft operations are gone from the REST API. In their place:

- **Generation renders a release.** Generate, batch, preview and data validation take
  `releaseVersion` to name a release, or `environmentId` for what an environment serves, and
  otherwise use the catalog's latest release; preview can render the working copy. Jobs, documents
  and collected results report `releaseVersion` instead of `versionId`. Data validation now checks
  the contract of that release and reports missing and invalid fields.
- **A working copy that is marked ready.** A variant's and a stencil's content is read and replaced
  through `/content`, and marked ready per variant, template, stencil or whole catalog. Every
  variant and stencil reports a status: new, modified, ready or released. Until the working copy
  loses its versions, marking ready publishes the draft, which is what a release reads.
- **Releases and deployments.** A catalog's releases can be listed and read, with their
  dependencies and the environments serving them; `/changes` lists what the next release would
  change. Environments deploy and undeploy releases over REST, with their history.
- **Held-back stencil instances.** Marking a stencil ready names the template variants still on an
  older version, and a stencil's usage listing says which are held back.
- `slug` is the only address of a resource; the deprecated `id` and `key` twins are gone.

MCP's `preview_document` and `analyze_template_data` take `releaseVersion` too. Collected results
store the release in a new nullable `generation_results.release_version` column.
