title: The catalog page is organised around your work, where it is live, and its history — with Fix this version.
---

A catalog's page now starts with **Your work**: what is not published yet, by name, and a Publish button. Then **Live** — which version each environment uses, with Make live and Take offline — then its **Contents** and its **History** of published versions, showing where each is live. From History you can **fix a version** that is live: the fix is a separate copy of that version, clearly marked, published as its next patch without your other work. Version numbers, other drafts, ready marks and dependencies are still there, folded under Advanced. The tenant's home page uses the same words.
