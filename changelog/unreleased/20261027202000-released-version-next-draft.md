title: The theme editor and stencil saves offer to open the next draft when the version was released.
---

When the catalog version a theme or a stencil is open in gets released meanwhile, saving is
refused, because a released version never changes. The theme editor and the template editor's
stencil saves now say so and offer to open the catalog's next draft, as the template editor
already did for its own saves.
