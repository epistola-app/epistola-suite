title: The outbox reports how many archive bytes it is holding.
---

A publication keeps its exact release ZIP until Exchange decides, and a `FAILED` one keeps it indefinitely so an administrator can retry — deliberate, and the only unbounded thing in the feature. `epistola.installation.exchange_publication_retained_archive_bytes` makes it visible instead of showing up as disk nothing accounts for.
