title: Getting to a first document takes fewer steps, and the screens say what they mean.
---

A new template opens in its editor, and the template page's main action is Open editor; Getting
started's "Create a template" opens the new-template dialog. The editor's bar shows a change as not
published as soon as it is saved, instead of saying "Everything is published" until a reload. A
template in a catalog that cannot be changed here opens in the editor to show how it is built (View in
editor), saves nothing and says so, instead of failing every autosave. Someone who belongs to one
tenant goes straight to it on signing in. The screens use one word per state: "Not published yet",
"In progress", "following its changes" and "published", not "unreleased", "Draft", "live" and
"released". The editor's bar names the variant by its title, the quality report names templates, and
the variants filter offers only the values its variants use. An empty Text block says what it takes,
and the palette says how Text differs from Rich Text Block.
