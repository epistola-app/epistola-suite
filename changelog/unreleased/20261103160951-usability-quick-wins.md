title: Getting to a first document takes fewer steps, and the screens say what they mean.
---

A new template opens on its data contract, which says why the data comes first and leads on to
describing it or to the editor; the editor's field picker links there when the contract has no fields
yet, and Getting started says so too. The template page's main action is Open editor. The editor's bar
shows a change as not published as soon as it is saved, instead of saying "Everything is published"
until a reload. A template in a catalog that cannot be changed here opens in the editor to show how it
is built (View in editor), read-only: nothing to add, nothing to type into or restyle, and the bar says
why. Someone who belongs to one
tenant goes straight to it on signing in. The screens use one word per state: "Not published yet",
"In progress", "following its changes" and "published", not "unreleased", "Draft", "live" and
"released". The editor's bar names the variant by its title, the quality report names templates, and
the variants filter offers only the values its variants use. An empty Text block says what it takes,
and the palette says how Text differs from Rich Text Block.
