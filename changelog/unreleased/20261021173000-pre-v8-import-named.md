title: A catalog archive exported from 1.x imports under the registry and namespace you name.
---

Archives in wire v7 and earlier are migrated on import. The web UI asks for the registry and namespace, proposing `local` and the tenant's namespace, which is the name the upgrade gives the tenant's own catalogs; REST `importCatalog` takes `registry` and `namespace`. The catalogs the archive uses are declared and pinned to the releases the tenant has. A 1.x export labelled `-dev`, which was never released, is refused with an explanation of how to release it first or set its version by hand.
