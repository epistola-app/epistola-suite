---
type: feat
scopes: [support, catalog]
title: Tenants can share their released catalogs with Epistola for release checks (alpha).
---

With the catalog mirror granted and tenants named to Epistola, a tenant can share its released catalogs, which Epistola checks against new suite versions before it releases one. A tenant chooses every catalog or only the ones it marks; an installation administrator can preset or forbid it for every tenant. Only releases are sent, never drafts, with the releases they depend on, and what stops being shared is deleted at Epistola. `epistola.support.mirror.enabled=false` stops sending for the whole installation. See `docs/catalog-mirror.md`.
