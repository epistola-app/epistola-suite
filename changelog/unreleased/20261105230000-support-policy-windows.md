title: 1.5 is supported for three months after 2.0.0, and every release line has a fixed end.
---

Only the newest minor of a major is patched. When a new minor ships, the previous one stays supported for 30 days. When a new major ships, the last minor of the previous major, 1.5 for 2.0, stays supported for three months with security and data-loss fixes. End dates are published in the release metadata the version check reads, and may be extended but never brought forward. See `SUPPORT_POLICY.md`.
