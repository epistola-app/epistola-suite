title: The presentation dialog says when there are no images to choose.
---

Choosing a catalog icon or gallery opened a dialog with two pickers that had no options in them, and
nothing explaining why or where images come from. The catalog page now says the catalog has no images
yet and links to where they are added, and offers the **Change** control once there is at least one.
