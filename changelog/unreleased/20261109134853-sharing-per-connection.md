title: What a tenant shares is set per support connection.
---

A tenant's consent for support requests, the installation's preset for every tenant and whether sharing
is required or not allowed are now set per support connection. A tenant's Support page has a panel for
each partner connection next to the setup for Epistola support, and Platform → Support connections has the
preset per connection; choosing for one connection never changes another. Requests still go to Epistola
support only; sending them to a partner follows.
