title: The catalog pages scan better - environment cards, descriptions under their names, and text that stays in its box.
---

The Deployments page now shows one card per environment instead of a table: what each serves and since
when, its own Undeploy, the draft it follows, what it gets along with another catalog, and a deploy
block at the card's foot, with a short explanation beside "instead of the others"; the cards stack on
phones. The Themes and Stencils lists and the catalog's resources table show a description under the
name it belongs to, cut to one line with the full text on hover, instead of in a narrow column of its
own. A long catalog name or address in the dependency graph now ellipsises inside its box instead of
spilling over the neighbouring one. Card titles are the card's own 16px heading again, not the page's
larger heading with an empty strip beneath it.
