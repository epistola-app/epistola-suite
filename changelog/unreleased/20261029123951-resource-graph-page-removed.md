title: The Resource graph page is gone.
---

The tenant-wide resource graph explorer (alpha, behind `resource-graph`, off by default) is removed, together with its toggle. What it showed is where you work: **Used by** on a resource, a catalog's **Dependencies** page, and the Organise preview, which says what a move means for every catalog it touches. The reference data it drew stays and keeps serving organising catalogs and merges.
