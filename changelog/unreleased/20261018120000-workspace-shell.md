title: Every page says where you are, with a side nav for the catalog you work in.
---

The top menus are replaced by a context bar and a side nav. Inside a catalog, the bar reads **Acme / Letters / Next version**, with how many changes are not published yet and **Publish…**, on every page, including the template editor, where it takes one slim line. Switch catalogs from **Letters ▾**; the version menu holds the rest: other drafts, **Fix what is deployed to** an environment, and the published versions. Working on a fix turns the bar amber and says the next version is not affected. The side nav is the catalog's own: its overview, templates, the stencils, themes, images, fonts, attributes and code lists they are built from, and its **Versions**, **Deployments**, **Dependencies** and **Settings**, each now a page of its own. Versions say which version each started from; Dependencies says what a catalog depends on, which catalogs depend on it, and lets you add one; the overview opens with what the catalog is, then what is not published yet, then how many templates, stencils, themes and other parts it holds, each a link to its list. Outside a catalog — environments, settings, operations — the side nav lists your catalogs, and the bar offers the way back to the one you were in. Inside a catalog its lists show only its own resources — no catalog filter — followed by what the catalogs it depends on hold, and adding a template, stencil, theme, image, font, attribute or code list there adds it to that catalog without asking which. Environments now get a version by **deploying** it: Deploy, Undeploy and "deployed to" replace "make live" and "take offline".
