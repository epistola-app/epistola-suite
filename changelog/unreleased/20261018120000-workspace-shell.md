title: Every page says where you are, with a side nav for the catalog you work in.
---

The top menus are replaced by a context bar and a side nav. Inside a catalog, the bar reads **Acme / Letters / Next version**, with how many changes are not published yet and **Publish…**, on every page, including the template editor, where it takes one slim line. Switch catalogs from **Letters ▾**; the version menu holds the rest: other drafts, **Fix what is live in** an environment, and the published versions. Working on a fix turns the bar amber and says the next version is not affected. The side nav is the catalog's own: its overview, templates, the stencils, themes, images, fonts, attributes and code lists they are built from, and its **Versions**, **Live**, **Uses** and **Details**, each now a page of its own. Outside a catalog — environments, settings, operations — the side nav lists your catalogs, and the bar offers the way back to the one you were in. Adding a stencil, theme, image, font or code list from a catalog's list now adds it to that catalog.
