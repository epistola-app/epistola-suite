title: A catalog is named by its registry, namespace and key, in catalog wire format v8.
---

Two publishers' catalogs with the same key no longer collide: within a tenant a catalog is addressed as `namespace/key`, such as `acme/letters`. A tenant holds namespaces, granted by its Epistola Exchange connection or added under one of the organisation's domains by its administrator under **Settings, Authorities & namespaces**; a catalog it never publishes to Exchange can live under `local`. Once a release reaches Exchange a catalog's namespace is fixed. The upgrade names a tenant's catalogs `local/<tenant>/<key>`, and keeps the Exchange name of catalogs bound to or installed from Exchange. Wire v8 says `key` everywhere v7 said `slug`, names other catalogs by an alias, and fingerprints releases by content (`v6:…`).
