title: Catalogs move to wire format v8, named by registry and namespace.
---

A catalog is now named by a registry, a namespace and its key, so two publishers' catalogs with the same key no longer collide. Existing catalogs get the tenant's key as their namespace, which you can change until the catalog is published, and the bundled catalogs are named `epistola` on `exchange.epistola.app`. Wire v8 calls a resource's address its `key` everywhere it used to say `slug`, `themeId`, `stencilId` or `assetId`, gives every data example a key, and writes fingerprints with the algorithm that made them (`v5:…`). Catalog ZIP files from wire v7 and earlier still import: they are migrated on the way in and take the importing tenant's namespace. A catalog registered from a URL must be served in wire v8, because an older one names no namespace. The bundled catalogs ship in the new format: the system catalog as 2.0.0, the demo catalog as 6.1.0.
