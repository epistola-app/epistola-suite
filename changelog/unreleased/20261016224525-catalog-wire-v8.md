title: Catalogs move to wire format v8, named by registry and namespace.
---

A catalog is now named by a registry, a namespace and its key, so two publishers' catalogs with the same key no longer collide. Existing catalogs a tenant authored are named under `local`, with the tenant's key as their namespace (`local/acme/letters`), a name valid only within the organisation, which is all a catalog had until now; catalogs installed from or bound to Epistola Exchange keep their Exchange name, and the bundled catalogs are named `epistola` on `exchange.epistola.app`. Wire v8 calls a resource's address its `key` everywhere it used to say `slug`, `themeId`, `stencilId` or `assetId`, gives every data example a key, and writes fingerprints with the algorithm that made them (`v5:…`). Catalog ZIP files from wire v7 and earlier still import: they are migrated on the way in and are named as you say (proposed: `local` and the importing tenant's namespace). A catalog registered from a URL must be served in wire v8, because an older one names no namespace. The bundled catalogs ship in the new format: the system catalog as 2.0.0, the demo catalog as 6.1.0.
