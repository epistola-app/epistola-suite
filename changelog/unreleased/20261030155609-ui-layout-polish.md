title: Page headers, the What's New card, the cluster page and catalog lists no longer clip or overflow.
---

A page header with many actions (the catalogs list) cut its own title; the actions now wrap beside
it. The What's New card on a phone ran under "View details", the cluster page's tables ran past the
page's edge and wrote times in a second, locale-dependent format, and catalog lists cut their
"Supplied by Epistola" and presentation badges. A catalog's contents read "1 Theme" rather than
"1 Themes", a new catalog with nothing in it says so instead of showing an empty card, and the
installed-resources table no longer repeats the "Contents" heading above it.
