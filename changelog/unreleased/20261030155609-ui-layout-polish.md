title: A tidier interface that says what it means and why it refuses.
---

Row actions are labelled buttons instead of bare icons, labels read in sentence case, and each page has one primary action. Times show in your own time zone, with UTC on hover. Statuses, severities and roles read as words, not codes such as `IN_PROGRESS`. A form that refuses a save keeps what you typed and says why, text fields stop at the length the server accepts, and confirmed actions use the app's own dialog. The footer holds the installation's version, API docs, changelog and support status; your account is in the bar. Layouts no longer clip at ordinary widths.
