title: A catalog release can be exported exactly as it was released.
---

Exporting a catalog always gave you the working copy — labelled `-dev` when it had drifted, which
was honest but not what someone wanting the version they published is asking for. The export dialog
now offers the releases that retained their content, and exporting one hands over that release:
its resources as they were, carrying the version, timestamp and fingerprint it was cut with,
whatever has been edited since.

Releases cut before the suite retained release content cannot be rebuilt and are not offered.
