title: QR codes have alternative text for screen readers.
---

A QR code was tagged as an image without alternative text, which PDF/UA-1 does not allow. A QR code
that encodes a web link is now announced as "QR code: " followed by the link, the same information a
scan gives. Any other QR code, such as a payment code, is announced as "QR code", without reading its
data aloud.
