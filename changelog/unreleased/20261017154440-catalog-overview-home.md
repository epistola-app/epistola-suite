title: The tenant home shows where every catalog stands, and a help page and Getting started lead to a first document.
---

The home page lists every catalog with what is not published yet, its latest release and what each environment serves, and names every environment that serves nothing and so refuses document requests. Until a tenant has made its first document, **Getting started** walks through it: create a template, publish, deploy and make a document. **Help**, in the bar, explains how Epistola works and the words the screens use. An environment's page can **Try a document**: make a PDF of any deployed template with its example data, as an application would get it. A new template opens on its data contract, and someone in one tenant goes straight to it on signing in.
