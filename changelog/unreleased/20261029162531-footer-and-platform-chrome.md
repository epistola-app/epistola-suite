title: The footer keeps product chrome only; your account lives in the bar.
---

In 1.x the footer repeated your name and a Log out button next to the account menu in the bar. The
footer now holds what belongs to the installation — its version, API docs, the changelog and the
support status — keeps its full width under the page, and wraps into two lines on phones instead of
squeezing. The footer no longer repeats your name, profile link or Log out; the account menu in the
bar is the one place for them. The platform pages (tenants, site banner, namespaces and shared with
Epistola) render with the same bar and footer as every other page, with a Platform menu to move
between them.
