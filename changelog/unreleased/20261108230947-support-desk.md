title: The support desk handles support requests in the installation, and passes them on to Epistola.
---

Platform → **Support requests** is the installation's own support desk: every tenant's support
requests in one inbox, filtered by status, priority, tenant and who handles them. On a request the
desk replies, writes **internal notes** that only it sees and that never leave the installation, sets
the status and the final priority, and **passes it on** to Epistola support when the tenant shares
support requests. A new platform role, **support desk** (`SUPPORT_DESK`, identity-provider group
`/epistola/platform/support-desk`), works the desk; who handles new requests stays the installation
administrator's to set, under Platform → Epistola support. The desk's tenant filter offers every tenant
with requests.

Every request has one handler. **New requests stay in the inbox by default**; requests already sent to
Epistola support stay there (the upgrade gives each of them that handler). To send new requests to
Epistola support instead, choose "Passed on to Epistola support" under Platform → Epistola support → New support requests, or set
`epistola.support.requests.handled-by=epistola` (Helm `support.requests.handledBy: epistola`), which
applies until an administrator saves a choice. Only the handler sets the status and priority; the tenant
always closes its own request, or reopens one that is closed or resolved, and no longer picks any other
status.

The support request permissions are now `SUPPORT_REQUEST_SUBMIT` and `SUPPORT_REQUEST_HANDLE` (were
`FEEDBACK_SUBMIT` and `FEEDBACK_MANAGE`), as in epistola-contract 2.0.0-rc.1; the roles grant them as
before, so nothing changes unless you named them yourself. Replies from Epistola are read every minute
while they come in, backing off to every five minutes while nothing changes
(`epistola.support.requests.sync.polling.interval-ms`, now `60000`, and the new `max-interval-ms`,
`300000`).
