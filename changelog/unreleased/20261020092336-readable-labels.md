title: Quality, Feedback and profile pages show statuses, severities and roles as words, not as codes like IN_PROGRESS.
---

Badges, filters and form choices on the Quality and Feedback pages, and the roles on the profile page, showed constant names such as `IN_PROGRESS`, `WARNING` or `CONTENT_VIEWER`. They now read "In progress", "Warning", "Content viewer". Templates get them through a shared `#labels.of(...)`.
