type: feat
scopes: [support]
title: The support desk attaches a further support connection to a request.
---

Besides passing a request on, the support desk can attach another connection to it, such as a partner
consulting Epistola support. The attached connection gets the request and everything written on it
except internal notes, its replies appear in the request and reach the other parties, and it hears
every status change; only the handler sets status and priority. It can be detached again.
