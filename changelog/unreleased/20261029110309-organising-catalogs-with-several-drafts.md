title: Renaming and moving resources between catalogs is stable and on by default.
---

**Rename…** and **Move to another catalog…** are on every resource, and **Create a shared catalog…** is on the catalogs list. A move updates everything that uses the resource, and its preview says what each catalog's next release becomes and in which order to publish them; it also works while a catalog has parallel work. The `resource-relocation` toggle is on by default. The alpha resource graph page and its `resource-graph` toggle are removed: **Used by**, **Dependencies** and the move preview show what it did.
