title: What a tenant shares is on its Support page only; the Support request sharing page is gone.

The read-only Support request sharing page duplicated the Support page and covered only Epistola
support. It is removed, with its menu item and address. While a tenant shares support requests that
cannot be sent yet, its Support page now says why under that choice, for every support connection:
the connection waits to be activated, is paused or cannot be reached, or does not grant the service.
