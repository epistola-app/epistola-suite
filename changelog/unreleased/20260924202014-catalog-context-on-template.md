title: Every page says where you are, with a side nav for the catalog you work in and a page for each version.
---

The top menus give way to a context bar and a side nav. Inside a catalog the bar reads **Acme / Letters / Next version** on every page, the template editor included, with how many changes are not published yet and **Publish…**. You switch catalogs from the catalog's name, and the version menu holds your parallel work, **Start parallel work…** and **Try it on an environment…**. While you work in parallel work a banner says so, names the environments following it and leads back to the next version. A subscribed catalog shows that it can't be edited here. The side nav is the catalog's own: its overview, its templates, the stencils, themes, images, fonts, attributes and code lists they are built from, and its **Versions**, **Deployments**, **Dependencies** and **Settings** pages. The overview says what the catalog is, what is not published yet and what it holds, and for a new catalog the steps from an empty catalog to a document your application makes. Lists inside a catalog show its own resources, followed by what the catalogs it depends on hold, and adding a resource there adds it to that catalog without asking which. Every version has a page of its own: a published one says where it is deployed and offers export and the other actions on it. Outside a catalog — environments, settings, operations — the side nav lists your catalogs and the bar leads back to the one you were in.
