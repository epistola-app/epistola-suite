title: A catalog shows its release history.

A catalog page showed the version it was last released at and nothing about the releases behind it.
It now lists them, newest first, with when each was cut, what it contained and its notes — and
offers each one for export, exactly as it was released.

Releases cut before Epistola kept the content of a release say so, and are not offered: they cannot
be rebuilt, and an export of one would refuse.
