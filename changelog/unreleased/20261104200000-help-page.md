title: A help page explains how Epistola works and where to find things.
---

**Help**, in the bar at the top of every tenant page and in the account menu, opens a page for people
new to Epistola and for those who use it without knowing it well. It covers what Epistola does in four
steps (design, publish, deploy, generate) and the words the screens use. It shows the bar and the side
menu piece by piece, and answers common questions under _I want to…_, such as changing a letter,
letting applications use a change, trying a change first or going back to an earlier release. It also
walks through a first document. Places your role does not include are named without a link. Getting
started on the home page links to it.
