title: Quality findings are listed per catalog.

The quality report (alpha) is a catalog's page, at `/tenants/{tenant}/catalogs/{catalog}/quality`, and a finding's page is `…/quality/{finding}` in the catalog of its template. The report across every catalog, `/tenants/{tenant}/quality`, and its catalog filter are gone; raising a finding by hand offers the catalog's templates.
